		``The Watchman's Gone'' is the eighth song on Canadian singer-songwriter Gordon Lightfoot's album \emph{Sundown}. Largely overshadowed by other superlative tracks on the album --- like ``Sundown'' and ``Carefree Highway'' --- ``The Watchman's Gone'' is Lightfoot's allegorical, resigned narrative of his relationship to addiction.
